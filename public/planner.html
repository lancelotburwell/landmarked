How changes work

Every saved change is a commit to the game's files and goes live about a minute later. Clues, texts and photos change in place. Moving or retiring a building re-plans the schedule straight away: a locked day only swaps that one building, later days are redrawn. Instagram drafts already in Metricool are not changed; redo those there.
