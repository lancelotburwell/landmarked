How changes work

Every saved change is a commit to the game's files and goes live about a minute later. Clues, texts and photos change in place. Moving or retiring a building re-plans the schedule straight away: a locked day only swaps that one building, later days are redrawn. An Instagram post already rendered keeps its old images; delete them in public/social/ to re-render.
