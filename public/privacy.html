Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Short version: Landmarked has no accounts and sets no cookies. Your games and stats stay on your device. We count anonymous daily totals (how many people played, finished and shared), never who you are.

Who is responsible

Burwell Projects
Email: burwell.projects@gmail.com

This policy follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for visitors in the EU and UK, the GDPR.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress in your browser's local storage: the buildings and guesses of each day you play, your practice round, and a note of which anonymous counts (below) your device has already sent. Your streak and stats are calculated from this on your device. None of it is sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. Landmarked does not use cookies.

Anonymous statistics

To learn whether people enjoy the game, the site sends small anonymous events to our server: a visit, starting the day's game, finishing it (with the score), sharing a result, and starting a practice round. With a start we also send whether your device has played before and whether it finished the previous day's puzzle. With a visit we send the name of the website you came from, if any (for example reddit.com).

The server only adds these to totals for that day: counts, a score distribution and countries. Your country is looked up from your IP address by our hosting provider when the request arrives. We don't store your IP address, and we don't give your device an identifier, so we cannot tell you apart from other players or follow you from day to day. The totals are kept for up to 400 days. They are stored with Upstash, Inc. (database in Frankfurt, Germany).

These totals also power the line "You beat X% of today's players" at the end of a game.

Feedback

If you send a message with "Feedback straight to the creator" at the end of a game, we receive the text you wrote, together with the day you played, your score, and your country (looked up as above). We don't store your IP address. Please don't include personal details unless you want a reply; if you add your email address, we only use it to answer you. Messages are emailed to us through Resend, Inc. (USA) and a copy is kept with Upstash (Frankfurt, Germany), at most the latest 500 messages. Ask us at the email address above and we will delete yours.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). Like any web server, Vercel processes technical data for each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Vercel keeps such logs only for a short time. Transfers to the USA are covered by the EU–US and Swiss–US Data Privacy Frameworks and Vercel's standard contractual clauses.

Map imagery

The satellite imagery on the globe is loaded by your browser directly from NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov, USA), so NASA's servers receive your IP address and the map tiles requested, as with any website. See NASA's privacy policy. Building photos and fonts are served from our own site.

Links and sharing

Photo credits link to Wikimedia Commons and other sites; their own privacy policies apply once you follow a link. When you share a result, your device's share menu or clipboard is used. Nothing is posted anywhere on your behalf.

Advertising

Landmarked currently shows no ads. If that changes, this policy will be updated first, and any cookies or tracking that need your consent will only be used once you have given it.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. As described above, we don't store data that identifies you, so in practice there is usually nothing to hand over. Write to the email address above. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU, the authority in your country.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
