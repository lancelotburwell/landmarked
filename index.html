Same buildings for everyone. New ones at midnight.

How it works

  1. Every day there are 5 buildings, the same for everyone: two easy, one medium, two hard.
  2. Read the clue, then tap the globe where you think the building stands. Drag to spin, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  3. Want a second look? Tick Confirm location before locking in: your tap drops a pin, and Lock in makes it final.
  4. Each building is worth 200 points. Within 10 km you get them all; after that, points fall off with distance (half at about 565 km away).
  5. Best possible day: 1,000. Share your score and come back tomorrow.
