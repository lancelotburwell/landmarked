How it works

  1. Every day there are 5 buildings, the same for everyone. They start easy and get harder as you go.
  2. Read the clue, then tap the globe where you think the building stands. Drag to spin, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  3. Each building is worth 200 points. Within 10 km you get them all; after that, points fall off with distance (half at about 1,000 km away).
  4. Best possible day: 1,000. Share your score and come back tomorrow. Missed a day? You can catch up on the last five from the results screen.
